Hackett PMO Engagement Model
PMO support model designed to support full enterprise transformation · CRM / ERP / EPM / CLM / HCM  ·  Phase 0 & Phase 1 Focus
⬤ Initial Focus
Phase 0: Enterprise Program Initiation
Months 1 – 4
Phase Scope & Responsibilities
Program Setup & Governance: Guide and validate governance frameworks, establish program and project charters, define roles and decision structures
Performance Metrics: Recommend key, measurable metrics aligned to company and project specific objectives
Implementation Planning & Strategy: Assess and validate strategy, scope, and implementation plan; synergize workstreams and define readiness criteria for implementations
Agentic AI Accelerator: Define AI agent design for program monitoring & reporting
Phase Deliverables
  • RACI for program roles & responsibilities
  • Program KPIs and Reporting (incl. RAID, financials)
  • Advise implementation strategy & plan
  • Readiness Assessments & PMO Playbook
Org Chart Alignment
Program Manager Enterprise Program Governance RS&H ePMO Exec. Steering Committee
⬤ Enterprise & System Specific Support
Phase 1: Design Advisory & Governance
Months 5 – 9
Phase Scope & Responsibilities
Functional & Technology Advisory: Provide functional and industry expertise; act as a technology architecture review board across all workstreams
Requirements Validation: Validate design and solutions against requirements and leading practices; drive adherence to clean core design while accommodating BU-specific business needs
Agentic PMO Deployment: Deploy agentic solution and begin leveraging for reporting and monitoring
Executive Level Program Health Insights: Provide leadership level insights for risk elevation and health reporting / status management
Phase Deliverables
  • Review of design decisions and solutions
  • Supplemental design / decisioning documentation
  • Technology Architectural Summaries
  • PMO Agent deployed in production
